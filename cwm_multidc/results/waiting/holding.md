# Holding emissions: does waiting still pay when waiting has a cost?

Holding power P (W) is charged from submission until compute starts: at the source grid's CI until the data has arrived, then at the destination grid's CI while queued (`cwm_multidc/waiting_cost.py`). Every method pays it; every selection is re-derived from the saved evaluations (no new solver calls). Location metric, ε = 0.02 kWh/GB. Realistic storage for these 0.1–1 TB inputs is about 0.1–20 W; the top level is the whole cluster's idle power.
